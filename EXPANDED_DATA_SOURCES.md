## 🏛️ Comprehensive Historical Text Collection

This document outlines expanded data sources for training the London Historical LLM, covering a much broader range of texts from 1500-1850.

## 📚 Primary Data Sources

### 1. Project Gutenberg (Primary)
- **URL**: https://www.gutenberg.org/
- **Content**: 70,000+ public domain books
- **Time Period**: 1500-1850
- **Quality**: High (manually digitized)
- **Access**: Free, no restrictions

### 2. Internet Archive
- **URL**: https://archive.org/
- **Content**: Historical documents, newspapers, books
- **Time Period**: 1500-1850
- **Quality**: Variable (some OCR errors)
- **Access**: Free, no restrictions

### 3. HathiTrust Digital Library
- **URL**: https://www.hathitrust.org/
- **Content**: Academic and research texts
- **Time Period**: 1500-1850
- **Quality**: High
- **Access**: Free for public domain works

### 4. British Library Digital Collections
- **URL**: https://www.bl.uk/
- **Content**: Historical manuscripts, books, newspapers
- **Time Period**: 1500-1850
- **Quality**: Very high
- **Access**: Free for public domain works

### 5. Early English Books Online (EEBO)
- **URL**: https://eebo.chadwyck.com/
- **Content**: Early printed books (1473-1700)
- **Time Period**: 1500-1700
- **Quality**: Very high
- **Access**: Subscription required (university access)
